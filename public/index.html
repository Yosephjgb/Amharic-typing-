Amharic typing speed test

How fast can you type ፊደል (Fidel)? Pick a time, start typing, and see your speed and accuracy when the clock runs out. No account needed.

How it works
  1. Read the highlighted word. Words are everyday Amharic, like ሰላም, ቡና and ወንዝ.
  2. Type its sound with your keyboard: the consonant, then the vowel. For example s + e = ሰ, l + a = ላ and s + e + l + a + m = ሰላም.
  3. It moves on by itself when the word is finished. A wrong key is ignored and lowers your accuracy. Backspace fixes the current word.
The vowel you add picks the form of the letter:

A consonant on its own gives the 6th form (ህ). Vowels: e (1st: He), u (2nd: Hu), i (3rd: Hi), a (4th: Ha), ie (5th: Hie), o (7th: Ho). Turn on Show hints if unsure.
